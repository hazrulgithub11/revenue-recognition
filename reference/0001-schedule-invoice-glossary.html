Schedule vs invoice (RTO)

Layers

LayerOwnsGRSB / Zoho
Contract Term, principal, rate, monthly cash cm_rent_to_own_contract
Amortisation Period Opening, finance income, payment, principal, closing, period status cm_rto_transactions (UI: RTO Transaction)
Invoice Customer billing document for period cash Zoho Books Invoice
Payment Cash application Customer Payment → link on period

Industry analogues

  • Loan / EMI ERPs — pre-build schedule → invoice per installment (closest to RTO).
  • SaaS billing — subscription + next bill date → invoice; no reducing-balance amort table.
  • Billing vs recognition — different clocks; don’t store amort only on the invoice.

UX rule

Show periods under the contract with a related list. Do not duplicate the same 36 rows into a contract Table.

Future invoice rule

Invoice ← Amortisation Period (one period → one invoice for full Payment Amount, MVP). Period Status: Pending → Invoiced → Paid.
